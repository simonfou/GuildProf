# GuildProf design

A guild-private "who can craft what". Every member runs the addon; it reads
the player's own professions and recipes, shares them with the guild over
addon messages, and shows the whole guild's crafting in one window, offline
members included. Target client: WoW Forever (interface 16001) only.

## Window (`/gp`, or the GuildProf tab of the guild frame)

Three columns:

1. **Professions**: the crafting professions (Alchemy, Blacksmithing,
   Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking, Tailoring, Cooking, First Aid,
   Mining), then, after a small gap, the gathering ones (Herbalism,
   Skinning, Fishing; Forever gives each a few crafts too). Each shows its
   number of members; the tooltip adds the number of known recipes.
2. **Members, then recipes** of the selected profession:
   - A foldable member section: every guild member with the profession,
     best skill first, with skill and recipe count. Click a member to show
     only their recipes (click again, or the recipes header, to clear);
     right-click to whisper.
   - The recipes at least one member knows, highest skill first, with a
     search box (recipe or item name) and the number of crafters per row.
     Hover: the item's tooltip (the spell's for enchants). Shift-click:
     link in chat.
3. **Detail** of the clicked recipe: icon, name, required skill, reagents
   (hover for their tooltips), and who can craft it, online first (green
   online, grey offline, white when the roster names them differently) with
   each crafter's skill level. Click a crafter to whisper them. The list
   shortens to the room left under the reagents.

**Guild frame tab.** Forever's guild frame is Retail's `CommunitiesFrame`
(814 x 426, side tabs Chat, Roster, Guild Benefits, Guild Info, Preferred
Play). A `RightSideTabTemplate` tab goes under the last visible one,
placed from a `hooksecurefunc` on `UpdateCommunitiesTabs` and shown only
while the Guild Info tab is (a guild is selected). Clicking it lays the
window over the guild frame at the same size (beside it when the guild
frame is collapsed); any Blizzard tab (`SetDisplayMode`) or closing the
guild frame hides it. Blizzard's functions are only hooked, never
replaced. `/gp` floats the window again at 820 x 500.

## Data

- **Records** (replicated): one per character, owned by that character:
  `owner -> { rev, data }`, `data` = `Codec.EncodeProfs`: per skill line,
  level, max, and the learned recipe spell IDs as a delta varint string
  (about 2-3 bytes per recipe).
- **Recipe table** (static): `Data/Recipes.lua`, generated by
  `tools/gen_recipes.py` from the DB2 tables SkillLineAbility,
  SpellReagents, SpellEffect of the installed build. Gives every recipe's
  profession, required skill, created item and reagents, so the window can
  show tooltips and reagents for recipes the viewer does not know. A
  recipe the table lacks (newer build) still shows, with its sender's
  profession and no reagents. Regenerate after a client patch.
- SavedVariables: `GuildProfDB` (account-wide, partitioned per guild so alts
  in different guilds never mix data), `GuildProfCharDB.me` (the server's
  form of this character's name).

Recipes come from two sources, merged:

- **At login, again 10, 30 and 90 s later, and on every spellbook, skill
  or recipe change** (`SPELLS_CHANGED`, `SKILL_LINES_CHANGED`,
  `NEW_RECIPE_LEARNED`): each recipe of the player's professions in the
  generated table is checked with `C_SpellBook.IsSpellKnown`, then
  `IsSpellKnownOrInSpellBook`. The spellbook can still be loading at the
  first scan of a session. A method is rejected for a profession when it
  errors, finds nothing, or calls known 90 % or more of a profession of
  over 20 recipes (it answers true for any spell). The data's minimum skill
  is no test: 13 Blacksmithing recipes have a minimum above the skill at
  which they turn grey (Coarse Sharpening Stone: minimum 75, grey at 55),
  so a known recipe can exceed it. The diag line compares the count with
  the last window scan (GP10).
- **From the profession window**, while it shows a
  profession (`C_TradeSkillUI`, Retail API on Forever: the frame opens on a
book page, and `TRADE_SKILL_*` fire once a profession tab is clicked);
levels come from `GetProfessions` at any time. A scan never replaces a
list with an empty one. Without a guild the addon keeps the player's own
data under the key `""` and sends nothing. Guild key = guild name only
(`GetGuildInfo`'s realm return arrives late on Forever).

Diagnostics: every scan writes one line to `GuildProfDB.diag` (`basic`,
`window`, `event`, `error`), shown by `/gp status` and readable on disk
after a `/reload`:
`WTF/Account/<account>/SavedVariables/GuildProf.lua` in the client folder.

## Sync

Goal: the common case costs almost nothing, and catching up costs only
what differs.

Every record carries `rev` (server time when it changed, always
increasing). Higher rev wins, so merging is order-independent.

- **Own change**: the owner broadcasts its record on GUILD (`R`),
  coalesced over 2 s. Everyone online stores it. On GUILD a record is
  accepted only from its owner.
- **Versions**: every `H` follows a `V<x.y.z>` (the TOC's `## Version`).
  A player who hears a newer version says so once in chat and puts an
  update row at the top of the window, which shows the download link
  selected for copying. When an older copy's `V` arrives, one player on
  the newest version answers with its own `V` after 1-5 s (suppressed when
  another answer is heard first), so the older copy learns of the update
  at login. An `H` without a `V` just before it comes from a copy older
  than these announcements. Versions are kept per guild in the
  SavedVariables: member tooltips and `/gp status` name the members on an
  older copy. Copies without `V` drop it like any unknown message type,
  so new message types stay compatible with older copies.
- **Guild only**: the server delivers the GUILD channel to guild members
  only, and whispers are accepted only from players heard on it this
  session. Every sync starts there (the newcomer's `H`, the peer's `A`),
  so a player outside the guild who whispers `B`, `N` or `R` gets no
  records out and puts none in.
- **Login**: after 3-6 s the player broadcasts `V` with its addon version,
  then `H` with one digest of its whole table (hash over `owner=rev`).
  Peers with the same digest stay silent: **an in-sync login costs two
  messages**.
- A peer whose digest differs waits a random 0.2-1.5 s, then answers `A`
  on GUILD unless another peer already did (suppression: normally one
  answer). Only a peer whose own table is current answers: after its own
  sync, or after 20 s in which nobody answered its `H`. Two players who
  logged in together with stale tables retry once after that window.
- The newcomer whispers that peer `B`: 16 bucket digests (owners hashed into
  16 buckets). The peer whispers back `M` with the `owner=rev` entries of
  the buckets that differ only. The newcomer sends the records it has newer
  (`R`) and asks for the ones it lacks (`N`); the peer whispers them (`R`).
  When the peer had the newcomer's **own** record stale, the newcomer
  broadcasts it on GUILD instead, since the others likely have it stale too.
- On demand: the **Sync now** button (and `/gp sync`) rescans the player's
  professions and sends `H`; opening the window does the same when no sync
  ran in the last 60 s.
- Every protocol step is traced with its time in `GuildProfDB.diag.sync`
  (last 80 lines).
  Whispers outside instances are not throttled by the client.
- Transport (`Comm`): bodies split into 240-byte parts at UTF-8 boundaries,
  `id.index.count:` header, reassembled per sender; GUILD paced at 8 burst +
  1/s, whispers at 10/s; throttled sends retried.
- Own name: the server echoes GUILD messages to their sender; the first echo
  gives the exact form others see (Forever gives `First Surname`),
  and the record is renamed and republished under it.
- **Departed members**: on each roster update (`GUILD_ROSTER_UPDATE`) the
  records of characters the roster no longer lists are removed. A roster is
  trusted only when it lists every member (a name for each of
  `GetNumGuildMembers()`) and names the player under the records'
  spelling; names match as given, with hyphens as spaces, or without a
  `-Realm` suffix. Nothing is removed when more than half of the records
  would go (a misread roster, not a departure), and a character heard on
  the guild channel this session is kept (it may have joined after the
  roster was read). During a sync a former member's record is neither
  asked for (`N`) nor accepted by whisper (`R`), so a player who missed
  the departure cannot bring it back. A character who rejoins republishes
  its record at its next login, like any record a peer lacks.
  `GuildProfDB.diag.roster` shows the roster read (count, trusted, first
  name) and `/gp status` the last removal.

Measured headless (`tests/run.lua`, 20 players, about 500 bytes per record):

| Case | GUILD | WHISPER | Bytes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Login, already in sync | 2 | 0 | ~30 |
| 3 logins after 6 records changed | 19 | 47 | 8.9 KB |
| New member, empty table | 3 | 51 | 9.7 KB |

Known limits, accepted for now:

- A member on a version without departed-member removal keeps those
  records: its digest differs, so each of its logins starts a sync that
  transfers nothing, until it updates.
- Two online members who both missed a change only meet at the next login
  of either one (there is no periodic resync).
- Any member can forward any record during a sync (guild-private trust).
- Forever trial and Veteran accounts cannot send addon messages at all.
